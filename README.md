This repository contains records of Nathan's experience at Bluestamp Engineering from June 22 to July 10 (2026). The project in question is a modified Assistive Smart Jacket, where Pro Micro arduino boards calculate the distance from an obstacle to the person wearing the device using recorded time of the sound waves travelling back and forth between ultrasonic sensors and obstacles. The Pro Micro then activates some form of stimuli (audible or tactile) at frequencies directly proportional to the distance an obstacle is from the user, providing a warning and navigation system for the visually impaired in a manner not interfering with their daily lives.
